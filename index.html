About个人简介

I am an associate professor in the Department of Geophysics at Shandong University of Science and Technology, where I also supervise master’s students. My work sits at the intersection of tectonics, geophysics and numerical modeling.

My recent research interests include lower-crustal domes in the northern South China Sea, the Hainan mantle plume, and lithospheric drips beneath the North China Craton. I try to bring observations, geophysical inversion and numerical modeling closely together, so that each provides strong constraints on the others and a way to test them. From 2025.11 to 2026.11, I spent a year as a visiting scientist at the GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences in Potsdam to learn more about rift modeling. There is still a great deal I am learning, and I am always glad to hear from students and colleagues interested in continental rifting, mantle plumes or basin evolution.

我是山东科技大学地球物理系副教授、硕士生导师，主要从事构造地质、地球物理与数值模拟的交叉研究。

近期主要关注南海北部下地壳穹隆、海南地幔柱以及华北克拉通岩石圈滴落等问题。研究中，我希望把观测、地球物理反演与数值模拟紧密结合，使三者相互约束、相互印证。2025年11月至2026年11月，我在德国亥姆霍兹波茨坦地学研究中心（GFZ）访学一年，从事裂谷数值模拟研究。欢迎对大陆张裂、地幔柱和盆地演化感兴趣的同学和同行交流合作。
